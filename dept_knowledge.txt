About KJSIT:
K J Somaiya Institute of Technology (KJSIT), also known as KJSIEIT, is an autonomous engineering college located at Somaiya Ayurvihar Complex, Eastern Express Highway, Near Everard Nagar, Sion (East), Mumbai - 400 022.
Contact: 91-22-44444408 / 44444403
Email: info.tech@somaiya.edu
Website: https://kjsit.somaiya.edu.in
KJSIT is affiliated to the University of Mumbai and approved by AICTE.
The college was established in 1983 and is part of the Somaiya Vidyavihar University campus.
 
Programmes Offered:
KJSIT offers the following undergraduate and postgraduate programmes:
- B.Tech in Computer Engineering (COMP) - 120 seats
- B.Tech in Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering (EXTC) - 120 seats
- B.Tech in Information Technology (IT) - 120 seats
- B.Tech in Artificial Intelligence and Data Science (AIDS) - 120 seats
- M.Tech in Artificial Intelligence
- PhD in Computer Engineering, Electronics and Telecommunication, Information Technology
 
Admission Process:
Students can get admission to KJSIT through the following process:
1. For First Year B.Tech: Admissions are based on MHT-CET or JEE Main scores through the Centralized Admission Process (CAP) conducted by the State Common Entrance Test Cell, Maharashtra.
2. For Direct Second Year (Lateral Entry): Diploma holders can apply through the Diploma to Degree (D2D) CAP rounds.
3. Students must register on the official Maharashtra CAP portal and fill their college preferences.
4. Merit lists are published and seat allotment is done round by round.
5. Required documents: 10th marksheet, 12th marksheet, leaving certificate, caste certificate (if applicable), MHT-CET or JEE scorecard, passport size photos, Aadhaar card.
6. For more details visit: https://kjsit.somaiya.edu.in/en/admission
7. Management quota seats are also available directly through the college.
How to take admission in KJSIT? Apply through MHT-CET CAP rounds on the Maharashtra state portal.
 
Attendance Policy:
75% attendance is compulsory for all students at KJSIT.
Students with less than 75% attendance will not be allowed to sit for university exams.
Attendance is tracked subject-wise every semester.
Medical leave or other genuine reasons may be considered for condonation by the HOD.
